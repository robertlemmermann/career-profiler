Judge the final answer:

1. It frames the task as a fit or gap analysis against the user's profile: it either reports which requirements the profile supports and which it does not, or it states it needs the profile (asks for it or offers to build it) and lists the posting's requirements that will be compared.
2. It names at least three requirements from the posting (B2B sales years, managing a sales team, quota, Salesforce, negotiation, travel, degree, MRO).
3. It does not write a resume or cover letter.
4. It does not invent the user's sales history.

Pass when all four hold.
