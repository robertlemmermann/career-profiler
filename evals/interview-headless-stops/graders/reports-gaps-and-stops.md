You see only the assistant's final answer. Check one thing: the answer states that an interview needs a person, lists what is missing to build a profile (for example identity, work history, achievements with numbers, skills, education, certifications) and stops.

Saying that nobody could answer during the unattended run, or that the interview needs an interactive session, counts as saying a person is needed. A list of the missing areas, whether written as topics or as the questions a later session will ask, counts as naming them.

PASS if it names at least three missing areas and stops. FAIL if it names fewer than three, does not say a person is needed, or carries on as if it could continue.
